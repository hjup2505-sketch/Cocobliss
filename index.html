The Cocobliss Chocolate Standard

Why Connoisseurs Choose Us

We reject shortcuts, artificial shelf-life enhancers, and industrial waxes. Pure cocoa butter, honest origins, and time-honored artisanal methods define every piece.

100% Single-Origin Cacao

Directly partnered with family micro-estates in Ghana, Ecuador, and Madagascar. Each bean is hand-sorted and traceable to its regional terroir.

Artisanal Granite Tempering

Slowly cooled and agitated on Italian marble slabs to align the crystal structures of pure cocoa butter, yielding our signature glass-like snap and velvety melt.

Pure, Clean Ingredients

Zero palm oils, zero artificial preservatives, and zero synthetic vanillin. Crafted exclusively with pure cocoa butter, fresh dairy cream, and unrefined raw sugars.

Fresh Daily Micro-Batches

We pour only what is needed each day. You never receive factory-stored stock—only fresh, vibrant truffles bursting with living cacao aromatics.

Bespoke Custom Gifting

Personalize your gifting with hand-stamped wax seals, custom printed sleeves, French grosgrain ribbons, and handwritten calligraphy message cards.

Eco-Conscious Packaging

Every keepsake box and wrapper is 100% recyclable, biodegradable, or reusable as luxury vanity storage, honoring the earth that produces our cacao.

Our Heritage

The Art of Chocolate, Reimagined.

Cocobliss Chocolate began not in a commercial kitchen, but in a small sunlit studio with a single vintage granite conching machine and an unyielding passion for honest cacao. We believed that modern confectioneries had forgotten the romance of slow chocolate—the patience required to coax out floral, fruity, and smoky notes naturally present in the cacao bean.

Today, our chocolatiers honor that foundational promise. We source small bags of beans directly from sustainable, fair-wage family farms. We roast at low temperatures, stone-grind for over 48 hours, and hand-dip every ganache center with delicate precision.
